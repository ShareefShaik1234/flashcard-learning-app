Why SmartFlash?

Built upon proven memory-reinforcement techniques to elevate your academic and interview preparation.

Active Recall & Spaced Repetition

SmartFlash encourages active recall by hiding the answer until you make a conscious effort to retrieve it. By marking flashcards as Learned or Difficult, the platform tracks your weak areas, allowing you to filter decks and drill down into the hardest concepts before exams.

Whether you are preparing for technical coding interviews, university viva examinations, or building a strong foundation in computer science core subjects, SmartFlash provides a visual and intuitive environment to keep your study sessions organized.

Key Features

Everything you need to succeed, built into a beautiful, lightweight interface.

Card Management

Easily create, search, edit, and delete flashcards. Categorize your cards by subject tags to keep your study decks tidy.
